# Help someone reach a reasoned decision

Decisioncraft starts with a conversation and ends with a choice people can explain.
The diagram, notes and agent handoff support that process.

| What the person does | What the tool keeps |
|---|---|
| Explain the choice and why it matters now | The decision, purpose, people affected and stakes |
| Agree what matters | Must-haves and other criteria, before assessing options |
| Find realistic alternatives | Options, including keeping things as they are when that is realistic |
| Choose enough checking for this decision | A discussion, research, a small trial or another method |
| Compare the options fairly | Reasons, evidence, tradeoffs and what is still unknown |
| Make and explain the choice | The owner's decision, reason and remaining risks |
| Put the choice into action and check it | Proposed stories, acceptance criteria, a proposed-state map and a reason to revisit |

Start with four short questions. Ask about criteria, how to compare and the stakes as
follow-ups. Reuse information already supplied. A reversible everyday choice may need
only a brief comparison. A costly or hard-to-reverse choice may need a trial or more
research. Do not invent a score, a consensus or a final choice.

The interface shows Compare the options in plain words, alongside the diagrams and
people's perspectives. The host receives the same comparison and missing points in the
completion handoff. It then explains the answers, prepares missing stories and checks,
and asks the owner to resolve points that still affect the choice.

## Basis for the design

This is our plain-language translation of decision analysis and resolution, informed by
[CMMI Institute's July 2013 guidance](https://cmmiinstitute.com/getattachment/d75fe24a-9fe9-4b8b-a215-867b0554ef49/2013-07-July-Quality-Tip-Decision-Analysis-and-Resolution.pdf)
and the [SEI's publicly available decision-analysis guidance](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/documents/1849/2006_003_001_14645.pdf).
The first explains the broad range of decisions and the value of documented criteria,
alternatives and transparent reasons. The second describes criteria, alternatives,
evaluation methods and selecting a solution, with the amount of formality matched to
circumstances. Turning a selected proposal into stories and checks is Decisioncraft's
handoff to implementation.
