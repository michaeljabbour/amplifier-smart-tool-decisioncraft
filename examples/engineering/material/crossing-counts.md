# Crossing counts, automatic counter, two weeks in June (fictional)

- Average 1,900 crossings a day on school days, 1,100 at weekends.
- Peaks: 8:15 to 8:50 and 15:05 to 15:40 on school days, about 260 crossings in each peak.
- 22% of crossings are cycles; at peaks, cycles and prams often wait for each other on the deck.
- About 40 wheelchair or mobility scooter crossings a day, mostly from the south side.
- Counts in the school summer holidays last year were 45% lower than in term time.
