# Public drop-in session, community hall (fictional)

About 60 people came. Notes from the comment cards:

- Parent with a pram: "It's the only step-free way to the school. A long closure means a
  20-minute walk round by the main road."
- Regular cyclist: "It's too narrow to pass anyone. If you're rebuilding, make it wider."
- Shop owner on the south side: "Last time it shut for a month, my takings dropped by a
  third."
- Wheelchair user: "The ramps at the north end are too steep for me on my own."
- Several people asked whether the old bridge could stay open while a new one is built.
