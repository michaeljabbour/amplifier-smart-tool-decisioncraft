# Principal inspection, Riverside footbridge (fictional)

- Steel truss footbridge, built 1974, 48 m span over the river.
- Corrosion at 6 of 14 lower joints; two show section loss over 20%.
- Deck boards: about 30% need replacing within two years.
- Load rating today: safe for pedestrians and cycles; not for maintenance vehicles.
- Inspector: "With repairs, another 25 years is realistic. Without them, we would
  recommend a weight limit within three years."
- Bearings at the north end have seized; the bridge cannot expand freely in summer.
