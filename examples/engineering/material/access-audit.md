# Access audit of the ramps, June (fictional)

- North ramp: slope 1 in 10 over 14 m with no rest landing. Standard for new ramps: no
  steeper than 1 in 15 for this length, with a level landing every 10 m.
- South ramp: 1 in 16, with one landing. Meets the standard.
- Handrails on one side only on the north ramp.
- Lighting: two of six lamps on the north approach do not work.
- Rebuilding the north ramp to 1 in 15 needs 7 m more length, which only fits if the ramp turns
  back on itself.
