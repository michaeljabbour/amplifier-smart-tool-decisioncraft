# Short survey of south-side shops (fictional)

Eight of eleven shops replied.

- Six of eight said more than half their weekday customers arrive on foot over the bridge.
- During the four-week closure three years ago, five of eight said takings fell by a quarter or more.
- Asked what would help most: clear signs to the shops (7), a shuttle stop outside (4),
  a business rates holiday (5), works in summer rather than winter (6).
