# Ways across during a closure, early quotes (fictional)

- Temporary steel bridge, hired, step-free, 2 m wide: £380k for 9 months including putting
  it up and taking it down. Needs its own river authority notice (6 months).
- Minibus shuttle at school times only, round the road bridge: £1,450 a week, about 12
  minutes each way, 16 seats, one wheelchair space.
- Signed walking detour by the main road: 20 minutes longer; two crossings of a busy road.
- The road bridge footway is 1.4 m wide; two prams cannot pass.
