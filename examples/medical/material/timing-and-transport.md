# Timing on discharge day, two weeks of observation (fictional)

Invented data for a worked example. Not medical advice; no real patient information.

- Average time from "can go home" at the ward round to medicines arriving on the ward: 5 hours 40 minutes.
- Medicines for discharge reach the pharmacy queue in a batch after the ward round, mostly
  between 12 pm and 2 pm.
- Hospital transport must be booked by 11 am for a same-day journey; 1 in 5 patients needs it.
- On 9 days out of 10 observed, the discharge lounge had free chairs before noon and was full after 3 pm.
- Weekend discharges are about half of a weekday's, partly because no pharmacist is on the ward.
