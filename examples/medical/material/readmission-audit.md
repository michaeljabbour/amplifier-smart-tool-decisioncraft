# Ward audit: readmissions within 30 days (fictional)

This is invented data for a worked example. It is not medical advice and contains no
real patient information.

- Ward: a 28-bed general medical ward. 412 discharges in the last six months.
- 58 patients (14%) were readmitted within 30 days.
- In 21 of those 58, the notes record a problem with medicines at home: a dose
  misunderstood, a prescription not collected, or an old medicine still being taken.
- 63% of discharges happen after 4 pm. The pharmacy closes at 5:30 pm.
- Discharge letters reached the family doctor within 24 hours for 71% of patients.
