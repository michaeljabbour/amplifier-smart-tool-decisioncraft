# Two-week try-out of follow-up calls (fictional)

Invented for a worked example.

- The discharge coordinator called 31 patients within two working days.
- 23 answered on the first try; 5 more on the second; 3 never answered.
- 9 of 28 had a question about medicines; 4 were sent back to the pharmacist the same day.
- Average call: 7 minutes. Calls in the afternoon were answered more often than in the morning.
- Coordinator: "I could only do this because two planned meetings were cancelled."
