# Patient and carer feedback, summarised (fictional)

Invented quotes for a worked example; no real people.

- Patient, 78: "Everyone was kind, but I left with a bag of tablets and no idea which
  were new."
- Carer for her father: "We were told he'd go home in the morning. We waited until
  7 pm for his medicines."
- Patient, 64: "Nobody rang me afterwards. I didn't know who to call when I felt worse."
- Carer: "A simple sheet saying what changed would have helped more than anything."
