# Language and access, ward records sample (fictional)

Invented for a worked example.

- About 8% of patients on the ward need an interpreter; telephone interpreting is booked through
  a central line and usually takes 20 to 40 minutes to connect.
- The current discharge papers are printed in English only, in small type.
- About 1 in 4 patients go home to live alone.
- Patient group comment: "Large print and a picture of each tablet would help my mum."
