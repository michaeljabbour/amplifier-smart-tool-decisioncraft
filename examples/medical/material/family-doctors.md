# What local family doctor practices said about discharge letters (fictional)

Invented for a worked example. Phone calls with five practices.

- Four of five said the most useful part of the letter is "what changed with medicines and why".
- Three of five said letters sometimes arrive after the patient has already phoned them.
- Two practices asked for one named contact on the ward for questions in the first week.
- Practices read letters in the order they arrive; there is no flag for "needs action soon".
