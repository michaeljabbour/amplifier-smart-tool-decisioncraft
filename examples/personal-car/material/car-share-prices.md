# Car share and other ways to get around (fictional plan, real price ranges)

- Nearest car share spot: a 15-minute walk. Typical published prices in 2025–26:
  about $9 a month or $90 a year to join, roughly $9 to $11 an hour or $66 to $89 a day,
  fuel and insurance included (Ridester, Zipcar pricing guide, 2026).
- Dana's estimate if car-free: about 5 hours a week of car share for shopping and
  weekend trips (about $2,860 a year), 6 day hires a year (about $510), plus taxis,
  ride hire and a bus pass (about $3,000 a year).
- The school run cannot use the car share: the spot is often empty at 8 a.m., and two
  child seats would have to be carried there and back every day.
