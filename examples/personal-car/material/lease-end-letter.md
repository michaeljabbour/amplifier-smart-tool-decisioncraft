# Lease-end letter (fictional)

From the leasing company, 2 September 2026, to Dana (fictional), about a 2022 compact SUV.

- Your lease ends on 15 March 2027. You have three choices: return the car, buy it, or
  lease a new one with us.
- Buyout price at the end of the lease: $21,400, plus a $350 purchase fee and sales tax.
- Current payment: $485 a month. Miles allowed: 12,000 a year. Miles so far: 41,200 of
  48,000.
- If you return it: a $395 return fee, plus charges for wear beyond normal (we will
  inspect it 30 days before the end).
- Our current offer on a new lease of the 2027 model: $520 a month for 36 months,
  $2,500 due at signing, 12,000 miles a year.
