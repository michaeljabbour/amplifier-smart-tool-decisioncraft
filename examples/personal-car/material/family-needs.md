# What the family needs (fictional)

Kitchen-table talk, 27 September 2026.

- School run every weekday, two children aged 4 and 7, both in child seats.
- Dana drives about 12,000 miles a year; the commute might grow if a job change happens
  next year ("maybe twice the distance").
- Monthly budget for the car, all in: no more than $650 if we can help it.
- Partner: "I don't want another three-year lease cycle. I want to stop paying for
  something we never own."
- Dana: "I don't want to be stuck at the roadside with the kids. Reliability matters
  more than a new-car smell."
- Weekend trips to grandparents, 180 miles each way, about once a month.
