# Harbourside Bike Hire

A small web app where visitors book a bike for the day and staff check bikes in and out.

## How a booking works

1. A visitor picks a date and a bike size on the booking page.
2. The app checks there is a free bike of that size and holds it for 15 minutes.
3. The visitor pays at the counter when they arrive; there is no online payment yet.
4. Staff mark the bike as out, then as back in at the end of the day.

Known problems: people book and never turn up, so held bikes sit unused. There is no reminder
message, and staff keep a paper list of damaged bikes.
