# What other local food boxes do (fictional)

Checked three local boxes by their websites and one phone call, March.

- A cheese shop box: £32 a month, pick-up only, skip any month online before the 20th.
- A veg box: £18 a week, delivery only, "pause up to four weeks a year".
- A coffee roaster: £14 a fortnight, posted, cancel any time, tells you what is coming next
  week by email every Friday.
- All three show a photo of "this month's box" on the front page.
- None of the three offer an allergy swap. The cheese shop says "we can't guarantee".
