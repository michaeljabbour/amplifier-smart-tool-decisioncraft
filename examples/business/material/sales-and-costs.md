# Sales and costs, last 12 months (fictional)

- Three shops. Average weekend sales are 2.4 times weekday sales.
- Roughly 380 customers visit at least once a week (loyalty card data).
- Waste: 9% of baked goods are thrown away or given away at closing.
- A trial box (sourdough loaf, 4 pastries, jar of jam, biscuits) costs £11.40 in
  ingredients and packaging, plus about 14 minutes of staff time to pack.
- Card fees are 1.7% per charge. A small-business subscription service quoted 3% per charge.
- Local courier quote: £4.20 per drop within 3 miles, Tuesday or Friday only.
