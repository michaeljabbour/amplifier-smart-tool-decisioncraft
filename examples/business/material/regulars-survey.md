# Short survey of regulars, Mill Lane and Station Road (fictional)

Paper slips at both counters for one week in March. 64 slips back.

- 41 of 64 (64%) said they would "probably" or "definitely" try a monthly box.
- Of those 41: 29 prefer collecting, 12 prefer delivery.
- Price people ticked as "fair": £20 (9 people), £25 (24), £30 (6), £35 (2).
- 11 of 64 (17%) said they avoid gluten, nuts or dairy.
- 19 of 64 said they would want to skip at least one month a year.
- Free comments that came up more than once:
  - "Let me choose the bread." (7 slips)
  - "Text me, don't email me." (6 slips)
  - "Would buy one as a gift." (5 slips)
