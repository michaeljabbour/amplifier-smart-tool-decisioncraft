# Till system and payments, notes from the supplier call (fictional)

- The till can store a customer's card and charge it on a set day each month.
- If a stored-card charge fails, the till marks it "declined" in a daily report. It does not
  retry and does not tell the customer.
- The till can send a text through an add-on at 4p per message.
- Customer records in the till hold name, phone, email and loyalty points. Any staff login
  can see all of them.
- Exporting customers gives a spreadsheet with every field; there is no way to export only
  some fields.
- The till cannot raise invoices. The office buyer at Station Road is invoiced by hand
  in a word processor today, about 20 minutes a month.
