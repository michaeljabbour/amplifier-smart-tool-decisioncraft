# Customer chats, counter and phone (fictional)

Six short chats with regular customers at the Mill Lane and Station Road shops, March.

- Regular, Mill Lane, buys sourdough twice a week: "I'd pay for a box if I could skip a
  month when we're away. I hate being locked in."
- Regular, Station Road, buys for an office: "Friday treats for the team would be great,
  but I need an invoice, not a card charge."
- New customer, Mill Lane: "What's in it each month? I don't want six things I won't eat."
- Regular, Station Road: "Collecting is fine. Delivery would be nicer but I wouldn't pay
  much extra."
- Regular, Mill Lane, gluten-free: "If there's nothing for me, I'm out."
- Regular, Mill Lane: "£25 a month feels right. £35 feels like a treat I'd cancel."
