# Three Oaks Bakery: a monthly box?

**The decision:** Should our three bakeries offer a monthly subscription box, and if so, what shape should it take?

Three Oaks is a small chain of three bakeries. Regulars have asked for a box they can collect or have delivered each month. This canvas shows what a subscriber would go through, what the shop would have to do behind the counter, where the idea comes from, and what is still open. All names and numbers are made up.

**How to read this:** Start with the list on the left. The first view follows a subscriber through signing up, a normal month, and asking for help or leaving. The second view shows the same box from behind the counter: baking, packing, collection and money. The third view traces the idea from what customers said to what we would launch, and whether it worked; switch between Today and Planned to see what exists and what we propose. Each box says whether it works today, partly works, is missing, or is planned. Notes from the owner, head baker, finance and others sit on the boxes they are about; each ends in a question for the team to answer.

_Checked against the sources on 2026-03-28. Checked against six customer chats, 64 survey slips, a four-week waste log, a year of sales and costs, the till supplier call, three other local boxes, and one staff meeting._

## Words we use

- **churn**: The share of subscribers who cancel in a given month.
- **margin**: What is left from the price after ingredients, packaging, fees and staff time.
- **pause**: Skipping a month without cancelling.
- **packing list**: The sheet that says how many boxes, of which size and with which swaps, to pack for a given day.
- **stored card**: A customer's card kept by the till so it can be charged again without them being there.
- **declined**: A card charge the bank refused, for example because the card expired.
