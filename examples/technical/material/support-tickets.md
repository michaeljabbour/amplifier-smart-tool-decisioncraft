# Support tickets about uploads, sample of 31 (fictional)

Tagged by support in the week after the 2 May incident.

- 19 of 31 were video uploads over 200 MB.
- 8 of 31 said the upload "got to 90% and then failed".
- 6 of 31 asked if they could "pick up where it stopped".
- 4 of 31 were from customers on hotel or train wifi.
- Quote, a wedding videographer: "I lost two hours re-uploading the same file three times."
- Quote, an agency producer: "Just show me how far along it is. I can't tell if it's stuck."
- Quote, a school office: "Our firewall blocks some sites. Will the new way still work here?"
