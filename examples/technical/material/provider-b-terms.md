# Provider B, call notes and draft terms (fictional)

- Free one-time transfer is "likely" for accounts over 100 TB but needs a written request and
  approval from their sales team, usually 2 to 3 weeks.
- Their bucket names are global; ours must be renamed.
- Deleted files are kept 7 days in a recycle area unless turned off. Our current provider keeps
  nothing after delete.
- Their upload completion messages can be sent to a web address we choose; they retry for 24
  hours if we do not answer.
- Their EU region is in a different country from our current one. Legal must check our
  customer contracts.
- Their onboarding team suggests writing new files to both providers for at least a month and
  comparing checksums on every file before switching reads.
