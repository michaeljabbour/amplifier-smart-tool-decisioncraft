# Upload numbers, April (fictional)

- 412,000 uploads in April. 3.1% were over 200 MB, but they were 71% of all bytes uploaded.
- Failure rate for uploads over 200 MB: 6.2% during busy hours (9am to 6pm), 1.4% at night.
- Median upload size: 3.8 MB. Largest: 9.6 GB.
- Upload servers spend about 55% of their time passing bytes to storage.
- 18% of files are downloaded more than 10 times; they make up 80% of data out.
- 9% of files are never downloaded after their first week.
